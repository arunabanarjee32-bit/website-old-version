# Product vision, positioning and users

## North star

Make earlier versions of the modern web **accessible, verifiable and understandable**—not just stored.

We want a unified, intuitive product that combines the best ideas of website archives, modern browser recorders, visual monitoring, and user-controlled backups. Long-term, aim to become a credible competitor based on experience and capture quality, not raw page count alone.

## Core users

- Individuals: save important page designs, discussions and moments; restore their own exports.
- Researchers, journalists, historians: source-backed historical snapshots with provenance and export.
- Developers/designers: compare interface changes and diagnose regressions.
- Teams/organizations: monitor public claims, product pages and policies with retention controls.
- AI historians and users: preserve UI history, feature availability and conversations they own.

## Differentiators to test (not proven)

- Capture and historic playback experience for modern JavaScript-heavy sites.
- Seamless private-to-public archive workflow with explicit approval and redaction.
- Visual diff + semantic/text diff + crawl health and replay-quality indicators.
- Personal AI product history without misleading 'retired model is still live' marketing.
- Open export formats; local-first and self-hostable options.
- Simpler adoption than stitching together multiple specialist tools.

## What VersionVault is not

- Not a way to recover proprietary server code, private databases, API keys, subscriptions or retired AI weights.
- Not an indiscriminate bypass for logins, DRM, paywalls or anti-bot systems.
- Not a guarantee of 100% complete replay for every website.
- Not an official OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or Internet Archive product.

## Working naming and business decisions

'VersionVault' is a working name; trademark and domain clearance have not been done. Licensing, pricing, ownership paperwork, public-domain strategy and product launch date are OPEN DECISIONS. Potential business models: free local manual capture, paid scheduling and private sync, business retention/alerts and enterprise managed hosting. These are hypotheses to validate with users.
