# Competitive landscape and benchmark matrix

**Purpose:** understand existing strengths, not to claim a factual single 'number-one' platform in every subcategory. Features and business terms can change; re-verify before investing or publishing comparisons.

| Platform | Public URL | Relevant strengths | Potential VersionVault differentiator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internet Archive Wayback Machine | https://web.archive.org/ | vast public web history, URL/date retrieval | personalized capture and modern-site quality UX |
| Archive-It | https://archive-it.org/ | institutional collections and managed crawling | simpler setup, personal/team entry path |
| ArchiveBox | https://archivebox.io/ | self-hosted multi-format preservation | cohesive capture + comparison + replay UX |
| Webrecorder / Browsertrix | https://webrecorder.net/ | browser-driven high-fidelity archiving | combine private history, monitoring and collaboration |
| changedetection.io | https://changedetection.io/ | scheduled monitoring and alerts | retain and replay full historic captures |
| OldWeb.today | https://oldweb.today/ | historical-browser emulation | modern accessible viewer + timeline |
| SingleFile | https://github.com/gildas-lormeau/SingleFile | portable static page saving | timeline and richer standardized archiving |

## Competitive claim policy

- 'Compete with' is a project intention, not feature parity.
- Do not claim best/number one without a defined independent benchmark.
- Test 30+ diverse, permissioned sites; publish reproducible methods and limitations.
- Compare capture fidelity, completed jobs, replay availability, price/GB, cost/snapshot, privacy, setup and portability.
- Archive scale alone is not a feasible early metric against the Wayback Machine.

## Initial market hypothesis

Win a narrow group first: developers, design teams, researchers and people preserving changes to modern interactive websites. Validate retention, willingness to pay and successful restore/extract workflows before expanding into a giant public archive.
