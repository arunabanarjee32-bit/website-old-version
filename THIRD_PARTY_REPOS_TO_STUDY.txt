REUSE STRATEGY
--------------
- One OWNED monorepo for original VersionVault code.
- Integrate libraries via package managers and services via well-defined APIs.
- Do not bulk-fork/merge all the repositories above.
- First benchmark ArchiveBox vs a minimal Playwright+Warcio pipeline.
- For replay benchmark SolrWayback; research others with license counsel.
- Make security, privacy, rights, maintainability and acceptance tests mandatory.
- Keep a dependency inventory with pinned commits, licenses and provenance.
